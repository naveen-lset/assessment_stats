Assessments · iPad v3

Three ways to do the vet’s job. All three share the same data (entries saved in one version show up in the others) and the same Assessment Masters.

VERSION AInlineRecord where you read.
  • Animals on the left, assessment rows on the right
  • Tap a row: the input opens in place
  • Save, then the next assessment that’s due opens
  • History slides in from the side
Best for: fast entry across many animals VERSION BWorkspaceLook first, then record.
  • Animal cards show what’s due
  • An animal’s workspace: its assessments and their trends
  • Add Entry opens a compact sheet
Best for: reviewing change over time VERSION CRoundsGuided, one step at a time.
  • Today’s list of who needs what
  • One assessment per screen, big input, last reading shown
  • Save & Next · Skip · Previous · summary
Best for: the daily ward round
